Shards is a single-player adventure game where you play as a small clay automaton. It is a PC game made in Unreal Engine 5.5.4.


Set off to discover a mysterious, hand-sculpted universe, solve small environmental puzzles, and uncover the secrets behind the origins of a ruined pottery civilization.

But don't let the fragile beauty of this world fool you: exploration will sometimes give way to danger. Regularly, you will find yourself trapped in closed combat arenas facing aggressive terracotta automatons. But why? This is where the gentle pace of the adventure shifts into strategic action, wrapped in a mystery waiting to be solved!
